ResidualSquares

An interactive least-squares lab: shrink residuals, balance the normal equations, and bend polynomial models.

Least-squares cheatsheet

Scalar view

Model
\(\hat y_i=b+mx_i\)
Residual
\(r_i=y_i-\hat y_i\)
Goal
Minimize \(\mathrm{SSE}(b,m)=\sum_i r_i^2\)
Picture
Square side \(=|r_i|\); area \(=r_i^2\)

Vector view

Design
\(X=[\,\mathbf{1}\;\mathbf{x}\,]\), \(\beta=(b,m)^\top\)
Prediction
\(\hat{\mathbf{y}}=X\beta\)
Projection
\(\hat{\mathbf{y}}=\mathrm{proj}_{\mathrm{col}(X)}(\mathbf{y})\)
Optimum
\(X^\top\mathbf{r}=\mathbf{0}\)
For a line
\(\sum_i r_i=0,\quad \sum_i x_i r_i=0\)

Quadratic: add \(\mathbf{x}^2\); also require \(\sum_i x_i^2r_i=0\).

How to play

1

Adjust. Move the pink model.

2

Aim. Shrink blue square area. Gauge mode: both dials to zero.

3

Lock. Green reveals the least-squares fit.

Dots = data · Pink = yours · Green = least-squares fit · Blue area = \(r_i^2\)

Medal = your total area ÷ minimum area · lower is better

Keys: B/M or C/B/A · arrows nudge · Shift = larger step

One lock per cloud · best result saved on this device
