Learning Goals
  • Choose the correct test for the interior, open edges, and corners.
  • Keep every valid candidate; reject points outside the region being searched.
  • Compare candidate values to identify the global (absolute) maximum and minimum.
Global Optimization Cheatsheet
  • Interior: solve fx = 0 and fy = 0; keep points strictly inside.
  • Open edges: fix one coordinate; differentiate in the moving variable; solve g′ = 0; keep points strictly between corners. Endpoints are corners.
  • Corners: evaluate f at all four corners.
  • Compare: evaluate f at every kept candidate; largest value = global maximum; smallest value = global minimum.
  • Avoid: do not solve f = 0. No second-derivative test is needed here.

How To Play

Each quest explores a quadratic function on a closed rectangle. Visit sites on the map, choose calculus runes, and pack every valid extremal candidate. Finish by identifying the absolute maximum and then the absolute minimum.

Full Mode: complete all nine sites.
Fast Mode: complete one trail or shrine with no misses to automatically clear the remaining sites of that type and pack every valid candidate.
