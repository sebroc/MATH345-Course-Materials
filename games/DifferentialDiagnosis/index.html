Differential diagnosis

Diagnose the mistake. Pick the treatment.

Learning goals

Practice recognizing common errors in multivariable differential calculus: domains and limits, partial derivatives, gradients and Hessians, Jacobians, the chain rule, and local linearization. Cases include mathematical statements and SymPy/NumPy code.

How to play

  1. 1Choose a mode below to start a 10-patient round.
  2. 2Read each chart and make the decision that mode asks for.
  3. 3First-try accuracy determines your score. Each round is worth up to 30 points.

Standard and Challenge award 3, 2, or 1 point per patient; a hint caps that patient at 1. Board Exam awards 3 or 0.
