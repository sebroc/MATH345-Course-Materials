About The Elimination Scratchpad

Purpose: This activity player loads a small augmented matrix from a textbook link and follows a goal-dependent sequence of Gaussian or Gauss–Jordan elimination questions, entirely through multiple choice.

Link builder: Opening the file without activity options opens the instructor tool. Adding ?author=1 also forces this view.
